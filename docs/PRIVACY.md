# Privacy Policy for Loksewa AI

**Last Updated: 2026-05-31**

## Introduction

Loksewa AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services for Nepal civil service exam preparation.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using Loksewa AI, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

## Data We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: When you register, we collect your email address, full name, and password (stored securely with encryption)
- **Study Progress**: We track your mock test attempts, scores, and categories studied to provide personalized learning
- **Reports**: When you submit a question report, we collect the report text, contact information (if provided), and device identifier (hashed)

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifier (hashed for security)
- **Usage Data**: Questions searched, tests taken, time spent on features
- **Scan History**: When you use the camera feature, the scanned text and matched questions are recorded locally on your device

## How We Use Your Data

### Core Functionality
- Authenticating your account and maintaining your session
- Providing offline-first question search and verified answers
- Tracking your study progress and generating statistics
- Delivering mock tests with timed assessments

### Improving Our Service
- Analyzing usage patterns to improve question matching accuracy
- Processing user reports to verify and improve question quality
- Training and improving our AI assistance features

### Communication
- Sending important updates about the app (with your consent)
- Responding to your inquiries and support requests
- Notifying you about new features or study materials

## Camera and OCR

Loksewa AI uses your device's camera to scan exam questions for instant answers.

**Important**: 
- Camera images are processed **on your device** using Google ML Kit text recognition
- Images are **never uploaded** to our servers unless you explicitly submit a report
- Scanned text is stored locally on your device for offline access
- You can disable camera access at any time through your device settings

## AI Answers and Disclaimers

Our app may provide AI-generated answers when verified database matches are not available.

**Please Note**:
- AI-generated answers are marked with a clear disclaimer: "AI Analysis - Verify with official sources"
- AI answers may be incomplete or inaccurate
- Always verify important facts with official sources such as Rajpatra (Nepal Gazette) or authoritative textbooks
- We are not responsible for decisions made based on unverified AI-generated content

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- Your verified questions and study progress are stored in a local database on your device
- This allows offline access to core study materials

### Server Storage
- Account information and authentication tokens are stored on our secure servers
- Mock test results and user statistics are synced to provide cross-device access

### Security Measures
- Passwords are hashed using PBKDF2-SHA256 with 260,000 iterations
- Authentication tokens are hashed before storage
- Device identifiers are hashed before transmission
- HTTPS encryption is required for all API communications

### Data Retention
- Account data is retained while your account is active
- You can request account deletion at any time
- Deleted accounts are purged within 30 days
- User reports are retained for 2 years for quality improvement

## Data Sharing

We **do not sell** your personal information to third parties.

### Shared with Third Parties
- **Authorized Reviewers**: User-submitted reports may be shared with content reviewers to improve question quality
- **Service Providers**: We use cloud hosting and analytics services that may process data under our instructions

### Legal Requirements
- We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal processes

## Your Rights

### Access and Control
- **View your data**: You can see your account details and study statistics
- **Update your data**: You can update your email, name, or password
- **Delete your account**: You can delete your account from the Profile screen

### Data Deletion Request
To request complete deletion of your data:
1. Go to Profile > Delete Account in the app
2. Or email us at privacy@loksewa.ai with your registered email

We will process deletion requests within 7 business days.

### Data Portability
You can request an export of your study data in JSON format.

## Children's Privacy

Loksewa AI is intended for users **13 years of age and older**.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete that information immediately.

## International Data Transfers

Loksewa AI is designed for users in Nepal. If you access the service from outside Nepal, your data may be transferred to and processed in Nepal, where data protection laws may differ from your local jurisdiction.

## Changes to This Policy

**Changes will be announced**:

Your continued use of Loksewa AI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
